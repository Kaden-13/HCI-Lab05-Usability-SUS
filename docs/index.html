System Usability Scale (SUS) Questionnaire

Weber State University class registration usability study

Before you begin

Please answer each statement based on the tasks you just completed in the class registration system.

Choose one number for every statement. 1 means Strongly Disagree and 5 means Strongly Agree. Go with your first reaction; there are no right or wrong answers.

This questionnaire rates the software, not you. It takes about 2 minutes. All 10 statements need an answer.

Keyboard users: press Tab to move between statements and use the arrow keys to change your rating within a statement.
